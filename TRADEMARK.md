# DecksmithOS Trademark Policy

The name **DecksmithOS** and the **DecksmithOS logo** are trademarks of the DecksmithOS project.

This policy governs how the DecksmithOS name and branding may be used.

---

## Allowed Uses

The DecksmithOS name and logo may be used for:
- personal installations
- educational use
- demonstrations
- community projects
- documentation referring to the DecksmithOS project

Attribution must be preserved.

Example:
Powered by DecksmithOS


---

## Restricted Uses

The DecksmithOS name or logo may **not** be used for:
- commercial products
- hardware devices sold with DecksmithOS preinstalled
- services or software distributions that imply official DecksmithOS endorsement

unless explicit permission has been granted.

---

## Forks and Derivative Works

Forks of the DecksmithOS source code are allowed under the Apache 2.0 license.

However, forks **must not use the DecksmithOS name or logo** if they:
- distribute modified versions
- are used in commercial hardware
- represent a separate project

Forks must adopt a different project name and branding.

---

## Example

Allowed:


CustomDJOS (based on DecksmithOS)


Not allowed:
- DecksmithOS Pro
- DecksmithOS DJ Edition
- DecksmithOS Hardware Console

without permission.

---

## Commercial Licensing

Companies interested in shipping hardware or software products with DecksmithOS branding should contact the project maintainers.

Commercial licensing may include:
- branding permissions
- certification programs
- official hardware compatibility programs
